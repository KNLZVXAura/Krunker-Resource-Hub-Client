# Krunker Resource Hub Website & Client Update v4.1.0
## New
- **Performance coach** (Dashboard > Diagnostics): suggests settings that cost FPS (CPU throttle, frame cap, FPS unlock, motion blur, instant replay, animated video skins) from the FPS statistics of your real matches. "Try it" changes one thing and measures the next three minutes of play against the half hour before it, on the same map when possible, then says in plain numbers whether it helped (FPS, 1% low) so you keep it or put it back with one click. Only settings that take effect at once are suggested. Needs FPS statistics on.
- **Auto clip at many kills** (Settings > Extras > Instant Replay): next to the clip at a kill streak, a clip is also saved by itself when you get N kills within a number of seconds (for example 5 in 10), even if you die in between. Needs Track Session Stats.
- **Discord webhook** (Settings > Extras > Instant Replay): automatic clips can be posted to your own Discord channel (the clip when it is under 9 MB, otherwise a short message), and the weekly card can be sent there too. The webhook address is a secret: it is hidden in problem reports and is never saved in backups or exported settings. Mentions are switched off.
- **Best regions for you** (Dashboard > Servers): from the pings of every region (and the usual ping at this hour from Ping History) it names the region or the few close ones worth searching in, and "Use the best regions" puts them into the matchmaker.
- **Userscript updates from the KRH website** (Settings > Extras > KRH Website, Dashboard > Userscripts): scripts added with "Use in KRH Client" on the website are remembered and checked for newer versions after start and every six hours. Ask me (default) shows the new version with the number of changed lines and any new warning (network, eval); Automatic updates untouched scripts by itself when the new version adds no network or eval code and takes the update back when the connection drops twice within half an hour; Off never looks. A script you edited is never overwritten without asking, one earlier version is kept for "Go back", and only KRH website addresses are contacted.
- **Weekly card** (Dashboard > History): a picture of your week (K/D against the week before, play time, sessions, most played map, a bar for every day) drawn on your PC. Save it as PNG, copy it, or send it to Discord.
- **Warm-up and tiredness** (Dashboard > History): the Play Log now also adds up kills and deaths by how long a session has lasted (first 15 minutes, 15-45, 45-90, after 90) and says when you start slower or your K/D drops in long sessions. Said only when the difference is clear.
- **Share weekly stats with KRH** (Settings > Extras > KRH Website, off by default): puts you on the weekly leaderboard on the KRH website (Community > Leaderboard): one small summary per week (minutes, sessions, kills, deaths, days played, days in a row, most played map). "Preview" shows the exact numbers. The client never holds a website login: the website asks the client for the numbers and sends them with your own login. The board ranks K/D and days played in a row, never play time. You can hide yourself or delete your stats on the website at any time.
- **Krunker updated: scripts that stopped working** (Dashboard > Userscripts, and a message in the game): the client fingerprints the game's own script files (their addresses and the ETag the server sends). When that changes and scripts that worked before start failing, a banner names them and offers the way out: check for newer versions from the KRH website, switch the failing ones off, or dismiss it. Scripts that were already broken before the update are not blamed, and after five days a change is not blamed any more. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Battery profile** (Settings > Battery, off by default): on a laptop running on its battery the client can lower the FPS cap (only when the cap can be changed while the game runs), switch instant replay off and switch motion blur off, and puts them back when you plug in. It only puts back what it changed itself, and only if you did not change that setting in the meantime.
- **The minute before a disconnect** (Settings > Connection Journal): every disconnect, ping spike and run of missing answers now keeps the pings and FPS of the minute before it, and says which side looks guilty when the picture is clear: the FPS fell first while the ping was fine (the PC, a script, heat) or the ping climbed first while the FPS was steady (the network or the server). It is also in the text you copy for your provider.
- **Did that change help?** (Dashboard > History): after you change your crosshair, sensitivity / FOV presets, auto profile rules or key binds, your K/D in the seven days before is compared with the play since then (never past the next change of that kind). It says how sure it can be: each side needs about 40 kills + deaths in at least 3 sessions, and a small difference is called unclear, not better, because K/D moves around by luck.
- **Setup bundles** (Settings > Extras > Share: "Share setup (bundle)"; website: Community > Setup bundles): one share code with your crosshair and a Krunker settings profile. On the website a bundle also lists up to 8 legal userscript posts, so "Use in KRH Client" applies the settings after you confirm in the client (undo in the settings history) and installs the scripts through the normal one-script flow. Scripts are never inside the code: they come from published legal script posts, checked again at the moment of use. You can also paste a bundle code into Settings > Extras > Share > Import.
- **Share ping with KRH** (Settings > Extras > KRH Website, off by default): the typical ping per region and hour of day from your Ping History and your time zone as a whole number of hours (no IP address, no server names, no location) feed the "Best region for players in your time zone" panel on the website's lobbies page. The panel shows a time zone only when at least 5 different players take part. "Preview" shows what would be sent.
- **Uninstaller**: every release now has an uninstaller next to the installers, `KRH.Client-4.1.0-Uninstaller.exe` for Windows and `KRH.Client-4.1.0-linux-uninstall.sh` for Linux. The Windows one finds a copy installed with Setup.exe or the .msi (for this user or all users), closes KRH Client if it is running and removes it; a portable copy has nothing to uninstall, but its data can still be removed. It asks whether your data goes too: **keep my data** (the default: settings, saved accounts, userscripts, statistics, backups), **delete my data** (everything in `%APPDATA%\krh-client`), and separately **also delete the recordings, clips and pictures** KRH Client saved in Videos, Pictures and Downloads (those are your own files, so never together with the first choice). There is a confirmation before any data is deleted, and folders you picked yourself are never touched. For scripts: `/S [/DELETEDATA] [/DELETEMEDIA] [/KEEPPROGRAM]`. The Linux script has the same choices, `--dry-run` to only show what it would remove, and `-y`, `--delete-data`, `--delete-media`, `--keep-program`, `--appimage FILE`. On macOS drag the app to the Trash (the README says where the data is).

## Changes
- **Play Log** keeps up to 200 rounds (was 50) and its file is written through a temporary file, so a crash while saving cannot damage it.
- **Connection Journal** only records the game connection, treats a clean close without a status code (leaving a game, hopping to another server) as leaving, learns a new usual ping when it stays high (for example after moving to another region) and starts over in another region.
- The play monitor no longer measures ping while the game window is hidden.
- `package-lock.json` is complete again: a plain `npm ci` works (it used to fail without `--legacy-peer-deps`).

## Fixes
- **Play Log** cut a running round every minute (each save closed it), so one match on one map showed up as many one-minute rounds, rounds shorter than a minute were lost and the 50-round limit was used up after about 50 minutes. A round now lasts as long as you stay on the map.
- **Connection Journal** kept flagging a steady, higher ping as a "spike" for the rest of the session after you moved to another region, and could hide real spikes inside that one event. It also counted one missing answer at the end of a match and one at the start of the next as "two in a row".
- Leaving a game or hopping to another server could be listed as a disconnect (a clean close without a status code), and a connection that was not the game's own could be listed too.
- If the installer could not be started after an update (antivirus, permissions) the client closed without saying anything; it now shows where the installer is.
- Release notes and release lists from GitHub could show damaged non-English characters when a character was split between two downloaded chunks.
- The 7-day graphs and the statistics export could skip or repeat a date around a clock change (daylight saving time).
- The "Copy For Provider" button of the Connection Journal said "Journal copied" even when the copy failed.
- The times of the automatic backups (before an update, scheduled) were shown shifted by your time zone: the file name holds UTC time and was read as local time.
- Restoring a backup now also refuses file names that Windows treats as devices (CON, PRN, AUX, NUL, COM1-9, LPT1-9, with or without an extension) and names ending in a dot or a space.
- The Network Doctor counted the step between its two test hosts (for example 15 ms and 45 ms) as jitter, which pushed it toward a false "your connection is unstable" answer.

## Notes
- **Website**: the weekly leaderboard needs migration `50_add_weekly_leaderboard.sql` on the KRH website, profile achievements `51`, setup bundles `52` and community ping `53` (all four are also in `setup_all.sql`; on an existing project run the missing ones in order). Without them the client works as before and the website simply shows that the feature is not set up.
- **Discord webhook** (4.1.0): treat the address like a password. It is never put in backups, exported settings or problem reports, so after restoring a backup you paste it again.
- **Share weekly stats**, the **auto clip at many kills** and the **warm-up insight** need Track Session Stats; the auto clip also needs Instant Replay.
- **Windows**: the builds (the uninstaller too) are not code-signed. On "Windows protected your PC" click **More info > Run anyway**. If the `.msi` does not install, right-click it > **Properties** > tick **Unblock** > **OK**; error 1603 / 1618: close other installers or restart Windows and uninstall an older KRH Client first.
- **macOS**: there is no signed build; build the DMG yourself or with GitHub Actions (see the README).
